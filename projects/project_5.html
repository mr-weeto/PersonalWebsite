Software Project: Paste Buddy Data Entry Tool

This tool was designed to make me more efficient at my job at a call center. I noticed that there were unecessary portions of time spent typing the same phrases over and over to customers in live chats and emails. There were canned responses and email templates that could be used, but the interfaces we used varied in usefulness across platforms. I created something that was portable, and suited the specific needs of that job and my coworkers quickly took a liking to the tool. The tool was written with vanilla CSS, HTML, and JavaScript so it could simply be emailed and saved as an HTML file on the computer. Last I checked, the department still uses the tool on a day to day basis so I updated the tool and sent it over. you can use the updated version at the link below or download it from my github.
