software: card based video game

Designed a digital real time card game inspired by the likes of "Taco, Cat, Goat, Cheese, Pizza
Objective: have the most points by the time the game ends
-Points are scored for each punch(1pt) or kick(2pts) on your side of the table.
-The first player to deplete their deck ends the game.
-You MUST start a combo by placing a dodge card on one of the three play areas,
  after which you can start a chain of like colored attacks
-You can use a like-colored block to stop an opponents combo until they place a guard break.
-you can play a power move on an OUTER-MOST play area to remove ALL the cards in that play area (including yours) from play.


Controls:
You have two sources of cards: your deck and discard pile. You may play cards from your deck into your discard or a play area. You may play cards from your discard into a play area.
Your deck, discard, and each play area has a key associated with them. To move a card, hold the key for the origin, the tap the key for the destination.

Player 1: left side of keyboard
Z - Deck
X - Discard
D - Play area 1
F - Play area 2
V - Play area 3

Player 2: right side of keyboard
/ - Deck
. - Discard
M - Play area 1
K - Play area 2
L - Play area 3
